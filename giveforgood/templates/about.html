About Rotaract BMSCE

Since its chartering in 2014 under the Rotary Club of Banashankari, the Rotaract Club of BMS College of Engineering has built a decade-long legacy of service, leadership, and community impact. Operating under R.I. District 3191, the club has grown into one of the most active Rotaract clubs in the region, driven by the belief that young people have the power to bring about meaningful change. Over the years, the club has touched lives through a range of initiatives spanning health, education, and community service such as Give for Good, a cancer fundraising drive, iTeach, a peer education programme, Rotech, an annual hackathon, Kali Kannada, a Kannada language outreach initiative, Khushiyon Ka Dabba, a weekly food redistribution drive, and Youth Parliament, a platform for civic engagement among students. Each initiative reflects the club's commitment to addressing real needs, on campus and beyond. Guided by the Rotaract motto of "Service Above Self", Rotaract BMSCE continues to inspire a generation of socially conscious leaders, carrying forward a legacy that grows stronger with every passing year.

Our Flagship Initiative

About GFG

Give for Good is the Rotaract Club of BMSCE's flagship cancer awareness and fundraising initiative, run annually to support underprivileged cancer patients with their medical expenses. Each edition brings together volunteers, students, and the wider community through a series of on-ground activities including walkathons, open mic sessions, flash mobs, and public fundraising drives alongside a dedicated website to ensure transparency and ease of contribution. The initiative concludes each year with a closing ceremony that celebrates volunteer efforts and features talks by medical professionals and awareness advocates.
